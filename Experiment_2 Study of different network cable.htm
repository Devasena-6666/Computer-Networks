EXPERIMENT – 2

Study of Different Types of Network Cables

Aim

To study different types of network cables, understand their construction, characteristics, advantages, disadvantages and applications, and learn the procedure for making Ethernet straight-through and crossover cables.

Objectives

1.  Understand different types of network cables.

2.  Identify the applications of different cables.

3.  Understand straight-through and crossover cable arrangements.

4.  Learn the basic procedure for terminating an Ethernet cable with an RJ45 connector.

Straight-Through Cable

A straight-through Ethernet cable has the same wiring standard at both ends. For example, T568B at both ends. It is commonly used to connect a PC to a switch or a router to a switch.

Crossover Cable

A crossover cable uses different wiring standards at the two ends. A common arrangement is T568A at one end and T568B at the other. It can be used for direct connection between similar Ethernet devices when auto-MDI/MDIX is not available.

Procedure for Making an Ethernet Cable

1.  Cut the required length of Ethernet cable.

2.  Strip approximately 1.5 cm of the outer cable jacket from each end.

3.  Untwist and arrange the wires according to the required T568A or T568B colour sequence.

4.  Straighten the wires and trim them evenly.

5.  Insert the wires into the RJ45 connector in the correct order.

6.  Insert the connector into the crimping tool and press firmly.

7.  Repeat the process at the other end. Use the same standard for straight-through and different standards for crossover.

8.  Test the completed cable using a cable tester or by connecting suitable network devices.

Observation

Different network cables were studied and compared based on their construction, advantages and applications. UTP, STP, coaxial and fibre optic cables were identified. Straight-through, crossover and patch cables were also studied. The procedure for arranging wires and terminating an RJ45 connector was understood.
